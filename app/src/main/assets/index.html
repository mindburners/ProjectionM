Tap Save, then a number, to store the whole layout. Tap a number to switch to it. On a laptop, keys 1 to 6 work too.

Sound

Sensitivity Shapes with "React to sound & MIDI" turned on pulse with the music the phone hears.

MIDI

Plug in a USB MIDI keyboard or controller Notes 48 to 53 (C3 to F3 on most keyboards) or program changes 1 to 6 switch scenes. Other notes flash the reacting shapes. Mod wheel (CC1) or volume (CC7) sets the brightness.
